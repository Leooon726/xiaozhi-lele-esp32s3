## Final prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: original character design for a small 320x240 / 240x320 IPS screen in a Chinese voice assistant named Lele. Create ONE adorable cartoon squirrel mascot, one single full-body character, centered and fully visible, isolated on a genuinely transparent background. A big round head, small soft round body, very large fluffy curled squirrel tail, warm chestnut-brown fur, cream muzzle and belly, small rounded tufted ears, large expressive dark eyes with simple highlights, a tiny dark nose, a friendly gentle closed-mouth smile and subtle rosy cheeks. Front-facing relaxed seated pose, little paws held close to the chest. Charming clean 2D cartoon illustration, bold smooth dark-brown outlines, simple large color shapes, very restrained cel shading, no fur microtexture. Must remain unmistakably a squirrel, cute and readable when reduced to roughly 140x140 pixels. High contrast silhouette suitable on both light and dark UI backgrounds. Keep the entire ears, paws and tail inside the image with comfortable transparent padding. No accessories, props, scenery, ground plane, cast shadow, text, lettering, watermark, logos, collage or additional characters. This is the neutral idle master design, suitable as the consistent reference for later listening, speaking and happy expressions.
